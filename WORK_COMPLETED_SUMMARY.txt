KEY TECHNICAL SPECIFICATIONS
================================================================================

FEE STRUCTURE:
- Default: 5 bps (0.05%) on deposit and withdrawal
- Range: 0-100 bps (0-1%)
- Storage: DataKey::LpFeeBps
- Admin-configurable via set_lp_fee_bps()

DEPOSIT MECHANISM:
gross_shares = sqrt(amount_a * amount_b) OR proportional_calculation
fee_shares = gross_shares * lp_fee_bps / 10000
net_shares = gross_shares - fee_shares
→ User receives net_shares
→ Full deposit stays in reserves
→ Result: Share value increases

WITHDRAWAL MECHANISM:
gross_payout = shares * reserves / total_shares
fee_amount = gross_payout * lp_fee_bps / 10000  
net_payout = gross_payout - fee_amount
→ User receives net_payout
→ Fees stay in reserves
→ Result: Remaining shares worth more

ECONOMIC IMPACT:
- JIT round-trip cost: ~0.10% (5 bps + 5 bps)
- Makes JIT unprofitable for small trades
- Long-term LPs earn from retained fees
- Passive yield from failed JIT attempts
